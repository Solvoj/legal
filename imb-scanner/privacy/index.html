Privacy Policy

Solvoj IMb Scanner for Android (com.solvoj.imb.android.app)
Published by the IMb Scanner development team · Last updated 25 October 2026

In short: The app itself does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Your readings never leave your device: everything the app does with them happens there, including the history, which you can delete at any time. The app's only connection is to Google Play, for buying IMb Scanner Pro. If you choose to answer our survey, your answers reach us through Google Forms.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the Solvoj IMb Scanner Android app distributed on Google Play (com.solvoj.imb.android.app) and nothing else. Separate web-based IMb tools published under the Solvoj name are not covered here and have their own terms; unlike this app, a web tool may receive files you upload to it.

What the app does

Solvoj IMb Scanner uses your device camera to read USPS Intelligent Mail Barcodes (IMb) printed on mail. Camera frames are analysed on your device to decode the barcode, and the decoded result is shown to you.

Information we collect

Only what you choose to send us, and, if you buy Pro, the order record Google Play gives every developer (see Buying IMb Scanner Pro). The app itself does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any personal or device information, and its own code makes no network connections. The only part of the app that uses the network is Google Play's billing library, for buying IMb Scanner Pro (below). If you answer our survey, your answers reach us through Google Forms (see Our survey).

Camera

The app needs camera access to read barcodes. Camera frames are processed in memory on your device, in real time, to detect and decode an Intelligent Mail Barcode. When a barcode is read and history is on, the app also keeps a small, low-resolution picture of the mailpiece around the barcode, so you can recognise the reading later (see below). Frames are not uploaded, and the app does not save photographs to your photo library.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a history of your readings: each decoded barcode, the date and time it was read, and a small picture of the mailpiece around the barcode. Because the picture may show part of an address, you are in control of it: you can delete a reading, clear the whole history, or pause history so new readings are not saved. The history stays on your device: it is excluded from Android's backups and from transfers to a new device. When you export, the app keeps the file in its private cache, so the app you share it with can read it, until your next export or until you clear history.

The app also stores in-app notifications, your preferences, and counts of how many barcodes you have scanned and on which days, which it uses to decide when to ask for a rating or invite you to the survey. All of this is kept in the app's private storage, is not readable by other apps, and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android Settings. If Android's backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include these, but never the history, in that backup. The backup is made by Android, not by the app, and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Buying IMb Scanner Pro

IMb Scanner Pro is an optional one-time purchase that adds tools for working with your history. It is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, under Google's terms and privacy policy, and we never see your payment details. To know whether Pro is unlocked, the app asks Google Play on your device; whether you own Pro is kept by Google Play, not by the app.

To talk to Google Play, the app includes Google's billing library. It needs the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions, and it may send Google diagnostic information about the purchase service, governed by Google's privacy policy. It does not send your readings or your history. Google Play gives us the order records it gives every developer who sells through it, which we use only to handle sales, refunds and accounts.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

  • The app contains no advertising and shows no ads.
  • The app does not use the Android Advertising ID.
  • The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting services of its own. Google's billing library may send Google diagnostics about purchases (see Buying IMb Scanner Pro).
  • The app does not track you across apps or websites.

Google Play provides us with aggregated, non-identifying statistics about the app itself — for example install counts and anonymised crash reports. These come from Google Play rather than from anything inside the app, we cannot use them to identify you, and they are governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Ratings and reviews

The app may ask Google Play to show its rating card, at most twice, a month or more apart, after you have used it on at least two days. Settings also has a "Rate IMb Scanner" link that opens the Google Play listing. Any rating or review you leave goes to Google Play, under Google's privacy policy; the app is not told whether you left one or what it says.

Our survey

For a limited time, the app invites you to answer a short survey about how you use it: from a card at the top of History, once you have used the app for a while, and from a row in Settings. Answering is optional, and the card goes away for good when you answer it or tap No thanks.

The survey is a Google Form that opens in your browser, not in the app. It does not ask you to sign in and does not record your Google account. To compare answers from different kinds of use, the app fills in a few answers on the form's last page:

  • the app's version;
  • whether you own IMb Scanner Pro;
  • how many barcodes you scanned in the last four weeks, and on how many days, as ranges;
  • whether you have opened the Pro offer;
  • how many barcodes you scanned with versions before 1.0, as a range.

They are never your readings, Mailer IDs, ZIP Codes or pictures. They travel in the form's web address, so Google receives them when the form opens. You can change or clear them before you submit, and we receive nothing unless you do.

At the end you can leave an email address if you are willing to talk to us about how you handle mail. We use it only to contact you about the survey, and delete it within three months of the survey closing.

Answers are kept in Google Forms, which stores them for us under Google's privacy policy. We use them only to decide how to improve the app, never sell or share them, and delete them within a year of the survey closing. If you left an email address and want your answers deleted sooner, write to hi@solvoj.com.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share personal information with anyone. Survey answers are stored for us by Google Forms (see Our survey). When you share a reading, Android passes its text to the app you choose; we do not receive it. The same goes for a CSV file of your history exported with Pro: Android passes it to the app you choose, and we do not receive it.

If you contact us for support

If you report a problem, we may ask you for a sample image of the mailpiece the app could not read, so we can reproduce the issue. Sending one is entirely voluntary and always your choice. Such an image may show a postal address, so please redact anything you would rather not share. Samples are used solely to diagnose the reported problem and are deleted once it is resolved; they are never sold, shared, or used for any other purpose.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised "last updated" date. Material changes will also be announced in the app's release notes.
