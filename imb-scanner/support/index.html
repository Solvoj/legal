IMb Scanner support

Solvoj IMb Scanner for Android and iPhone

IMb Scanner reads USPS Intelligent Mail barcodes (IMb) with your phone's camera, on Android and iPhone.

Get in touch: write to hi@solvoj.com with your question or problem, the app's version (Settings › Version) and your phone model. If a barcode won't read, a photo of it helps; redact any address you'd rather not share.

A barcode won't read

  • Fill the viewfinder with the barcode and hold the phone steady, level with the mail.
  • In dim light, turn on the flashlight.
  • The app reads only IMb, the barcode of tall and short bars. It doesn't read package tracking barcodes, POSTNET or other types.

IMb Scanner Pro on a new phone

Pro belongs to the store account you bought it with.

  • On Android, sign in to Google Play with that account and Pro unlocks by itself.
  • On iPhone, it usually unlocks by itself too. If it doesn't, open the Pro offer from Settings and tap Restore purchase.

A purchase made on Google Play doesn't carry over to iPhone, or the other way round.
